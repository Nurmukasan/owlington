## Advice used (verify before publishing, keep wording simple)

- Reading: questions first, then scan the text for synonyms; Not Given = no information; ~20 min per passage.
- Listening: predict the word type before the audio (number, name, place); speakers correct themselves; spelling counts.
- Writing: Task 2 counts twice as much as Task 1; clear position in the intro; 4 paragraphs, one idea each; Task 1 must have an overview (without one, Task Achievement is capped at 5).
- Speaking: no memorised answers; answer → why → example; Part 2 prep minute = a 3-word plan.
- Plan: all four skills every day; last two weeks timed full mocks; one rest day a week; sleep.
